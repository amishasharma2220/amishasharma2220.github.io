Hello!

I'm Amisha, and I code because I'm curious about everything. When something catches my eye, like how my university runs its club events, why smartwatches get heat wrong, or where a new restaurant should open, I want to know how it works. Then I build my own version.

I'm a final‑year CS student at Manipal University Jaipur. I like seeing an idea all the way through, from the database to the screen, until it's live and someone can actually use it. I'm also honest about my data: if it's synthetic, I say so, and if there isn't enough of it, I show that too.

Before all this, I spent two years as a freelance subject matter expert, writing more than 1,000 step‑by‑step STEM solutions.

Try HEATSHIELD in 10 seconds

Why heart rate misses heat strain

Smartwatches guess body heat from heart rate. Beta‑blockers keep the heart rate low, so the guess stays calm while the body overheats. Change the day and see who raises the alarm.

Patient takes a beta‑blocker?
